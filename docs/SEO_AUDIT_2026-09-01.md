# Supernova Dental SEO audit and delivery record

**Prepared:** 1 September 2026  
**Scope:** https://www.supernovadental.co.uk/, its current frontend repository, and user-provided Search Console, Analytics, Business Profile and anonymised lead exports.  
**Status:** Living audit. Source-code findings and completed safeguards are verified in the repository. Live deployment, rankings and crawl behaviour require post-deployment verification.

## 1. Executive summary

Supernova Dental has a strong branded search foundation and meaningful local visibility, but non-brand opportunity is concentrated around Bridgwater rather than an unqualified claim to all Somerset. The clearest commercial search gaps are private dentist, emergency dentist, Invisalign, teeth whitening and dental implants in Bridgwater.

The technical work completed to date removes several sources of avoidable indexation and trust risk: self-authored review/rating markup, duplicate canonicals, location-swapped recruitment URLs, missing sitemap profiles, redirect-hop internal links and broad same-day claims. It does not guarantee rankings.

The next workstream is clinical-content verification. Existing implant, emergency, finance and testimonial claims must be checked against current approved practice facts before they are expanded, re-used in schema or promoted in search snippets.

## 7. Implemented technical safeguards

- Removed published self-authored review and aggregate-rating markup. Google documents that local-business review snippets are for sites reviewing other businesses, not self-serving business review markup.
- Corrected canonicals on primary treatment and hub pages, then added explicit canonicals to current indexable utility/referral/careers pages.
- Added missing active team-profile URLs to the XML sitemap; current sitemap now has 47 URLs.
- Consolidated the 24 location-swapped associate-dentist URLs to the one genuine Bridgwater job URL and removed them from the sitemap.
- Converted current public pages away from duplicate `next/head` metadata to App Router metadata. Remaining `next/head` code is in retired/unrouted campaign components.
- Updated internal service/team links to canonical URLs to avoid redirect hops.
- Lazy-loaded the global footer map, simplified the floating navigation and removed Crazy Egg from the current source.
- Narrowed broad same-day claims to verified same-day emergency appointments in shared/campaign metadata.
- Removed unverified specialist/full-arch claims from published implant schema and an unverified service list from Smile Stories schema.

### Live SERP and competitor observation

The 1 September reproducible search sample confirms a mixed result type for priority discovery terms: Supernova pages, local-practice pages, directories and provider locators all appear. Named organic/local-market competitors observed in that sample include Cornhill Dental Practice, Lion House Dentistry, Quantock View Dental Centre, Parade Dental Practice, mydentist and the NHS Dental Access Centre. This is a sample, not a rank-tracker dataset or a Local Pack grid.

The live Supernova result also rendered legacy homepage content that differs from the audited source. That is evidence of deployment/indexing lag or live/source divergence, not confirmation that the current repository content is published. Priority after release: inspect live HTML, canonical, robots, structured data and the actual review wording before judging the effect of the completed work.

## 10. Local SEO, authority and conversion priorities

1. Reconcile name, address, telephone, opening hours, booking URL and review total across GBP, site and citations. Claim/correct stale directory listings before seeking new citations.
2. Use GBP categories/services only for verified services. Do not add keywords to the business name.
3. Add tagged GBP website, booking and service-menu links to distinguish GBP actions from organic sessions.
4. Build review request and response procedures consistent with Google policy; show a maintained “180+ Google reviews” reference rather than a manually updated exact total.
5. Pursue only genuine local prominence: local press, community partnerships, professional bodies, suppliers, relevant directories, original patient-helpful resources and unlinked mentions.
6. Improve conversion measurement: distinguish organic form submit, portal click, tap-to-call, WhatsApp, GBP action and attended appointment. Do not treat all events as qualified leads.

## 12. Measurement framework

Report monthly and compare with the equivalent prior period where seasonality allows:

- non-brand clicks, impressions, CTR and average position by query/page;
- priority-query visibility and top 3/10/20 distribution from a consistent rank tracker, labelled as tool estimates;
- GBP views, calls, direction requests, website/booking actions;
- organic form submits, calls, booking clicks, WhatsApp clicks and attended appointments where attribution permits;
- useful indexed URLs, exclusions, crawl errors, canonical selection and CWV status;
- referring domains and locally relevant links.

Forecasts should be scenario-based only after a verified baseline and approved content/authority plan exist. No rankings, traffic or enquiry volumes are guaranteed.
